# Ethics & Terms of Service

LocalApply is built on a simple principle: **automate the grunt work, keep a human
accountable.** That shapes every design decision.

## What LocalApply does

- Reads job postings from **public ATS APIs** the companies themselves publish.
- Fills application forms the way a fast, honest human would.
- Uses a **local** LLM so your resume and answers never leave your machine.
- **Stops at the submit button.** A human reviews and submits every application.

## What LocalApply deliberately does NOT do

- ❌ **No auto-submit.** The code has no path that clicks submit/finish. This is not a
  setting you can flip; it's absent by construction.
- ❌ **No captcha solving.** We don't defeat hCaptcha, reCAPTCHA, Turnstile, or any
  anti-abuse system. If a site shows one, that's a signal to stop and let the human act.
- ❌ **No scraping behind logins.** Discovery uses documented public board APIs, not
  authenticated scraping of LinkedIn/Indeed/etc.
- ❌ **No fabrication.** If the tool can't answer a question truthfully from your profile,
  it flags the field for you. It will not invent experience, skills, or credentials.
- ❌ **No mass spray.** Throttled by default; designed for tailored, reviewed applications.

## Why

1. **Account safety.** Automated submission at volume trips anti-bot systems and gets
   accounts restricted — the worst outcome during a job hunt.
2. **Honesty.** You sign these applications. Every answer must be one you can defend in an
   interview and stand behind legally.
3. **Terms of Service.** Auto-submitting and captcha-solving violate the ToS of most job
   platforms. Human-gated operation of public forms is a far more defensible posture — but
   **you** remain responsible for complying with each site's ToS.

## Your responsibilities

- Verify the accuracy of everything before you submit it.
- Respect each platform's Terms of Service and rate limits.
- Don't use LocalApply to impersonate others or misrepresent your qualifications.

If you want a fire-and-forget mass-apply bot, LocalApply is intentionally not that tool.
